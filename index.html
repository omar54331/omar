About Me

My passion for technology began at a young age when my father brought me my first computer, sparking a dream to build a career in tech. Today, as a second-year Artificial Intelligence student specializing in Cybersecurity, that foundation continues to shape my journey. Along the way, I discovered Software Testing and found my true calling—driven by a deep curiosity to understand how applications work, uncover edge cases, and ensure software performs reliably.

To turn this passion into professional expertise, I joined the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI), gaining practical experience across Manual and Automation Testing on real-world projects. I specialize in analyzing requirements, designing structured test cases, executing functional tests, automating scenarios, and identifying and reporting bugs. While Software Testing is my primary focus, I also explore Penetration and Security Testing to develop a comprehensive understanding of robust application security.
